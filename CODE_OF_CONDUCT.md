# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to help people understand and support children and teenagers. We expect
everyone who participates — through issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project
space — to interact in a way that reflects that purpose: respectfully, in good faith, and with
consideration for people from a wide range of cultural and professional backgrounds (parents,
teachers, psychologists, pediatricians, and teens themselves).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about content, sourcing, or
  technical approach.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about accuracy, clarity, or scope — not intent.
- Welcome contributions from people with different professional and cultural perspectives; this
  project explicitly aims to reflect more than one cultural lens.
- Give and receive feedback focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sharing private information about another person without consent.
- Deliberately submitting inaccurate, harmful, or misleading content, especially content that
  could put a child's safety or wellbeing at risk.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, code review,
discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue describing the
concern, or, if the concern involves a specific person and you'd prefer not to raise it
publicly, contact the project maintainer directly through the repository's contact channel.
Reports will be reviewed and handled as discreetly as the situation allows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct, and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that don't align with it, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
